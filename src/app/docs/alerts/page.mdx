# Alerts

Stay in control of the noise. Uptime Monitor makes it simple to choose who gets notified, how fast, and on which channel.

## Channels at a glance

### Email
- Enabled by default for every user.
- Ideal for personal notifications or small teams.
- Includes monitor name, current status, and a deep link back to the dashboard.

### Slack
- Perfect for on-call rotations or team war rooms.
- Send alerts to a shared channel or DM bot.
- Supports per-monitor overrides so each service can post to a dedicated room.

### Custom Webhooks
- Forward alerts to PagerDuty, Microsoft Teams, SMS gateways, or internal tooling.
- Each alert includes the monitor name, status, and relevant details so other systems can take action.
- Webhooks automatically retry if the destination is temporarily unavailable.

## Setting your defaults

1. Go to **Settings → Alerts**.
2. Add your preferred Slack channel or webhook URL.
3. Click **Send test alert** to be sure the message arrives.
4. Save—these defaults now apply to every new monitor.

## Fine-tuning per monitor

1. Open a monitor card and click the **bell** icon.
2. Decide whether to inherit workspace defaults or override them.
3. Choose different Slack channels or webhook URLs for especially noisy or critical services.
4. Use the **Pause alerts** toggle when you plan maintenance.

## Smart alerting

- A 30-minute cooldown prevents duplicate alerts for the same monitor.
- Alerts are sent when monitors go down and when they recover.

## Tips

- Route low-urgency monitors to a quieter channel so only mission-critical issues page the team.
- Pair Slack alerts with webhooks to integrate with your ticketing tools.
- Revisit your default channels quarterly—teams change, and alerts should follow the people who can fix them.
