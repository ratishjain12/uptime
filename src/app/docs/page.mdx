# What is Uptime Monitor?

Uptime Monitor keeps a constant eye on your websites and applications so you can focus on building, not babysitting dashboards. It combines simple monitors, instant alerts, and a friendly dashboard to let you know the moment something feels off.

## Ways to Use Uptime Monitor

### Keep Websites & APIs Available

- Watch landing pages, marketing sites, and APIs from multiple locations
- Track response times to catch slowdowns before they become outages
- Pause or resume monitoring whenever you need a maintenance window

### Understand Application Health with Logs

- Use the logger package to stream structured events from any service
- Choose the severity level that should trigger an alert (error-only, warnings, or everything)
- See log-triggered alerts alongside uptime checks on the same dashboard

### Stay Informed on Every Channel

- Email alerts for essentials
- Slack notifications for team rooms
- Custom webhooks when you need to plug into anything else

## Why teams rely on Uptime Monitor

- **Fast to adopt** – new monitors take seconds to create
- **Context-rich alerts** – who was impacted, what failed, and when it started
- **Status tracking** – monitor response times and uptime status
- **App Logger ready** – built for the logger package so application noise turns into clear signals
